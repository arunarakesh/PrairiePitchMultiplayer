Find your musical ear

What is Prairie Pitch?

Prairie Pitch is a relaxed, western-inspired way to practice hearing notes and major scales. Listen to friendly piano sounds, make your best guess, and build confidence one round at a time. Play with a partner at your own pace and earn points for correct answers.

🎵

Modes

Note Guess

Listen to one piano note and choose the note you heard.

Scale Scout

Listen to a major scale and choose which major scale you heard.

🌱

Levels

Both modes can be played at any of these levels:

Beginner

Natural notes in one octave (C4); no sharps or flats.

Intermediate

Natural notes across multiple octaves.

Advanced

Sharps and flats in the C4 octave.

Expert

Sharps and flats across all playable octaves.
